Meet the team.

Australian-built and founder-led. Two founders on every installation — when something needs attention, you talk to us, not a portal.

Noah Webb

Co-founder · CEO

Noah is the co-founder and CEO of Agent Hatchers. He looks after the client side of the business — new work, partnerships, and the relationship on every engagement — and he's the person you'll actually deal with. He also keeps across every major AI release, so when a better model comes out, your agents get it, usually within the week.

Before Agent Hatchers, Noah spent several years in property finance and data, then moved into family-office investing. He studied at Monash, is based in Melbourne, and has volunteered with Flying Fox since 2019 — these days he also sits on its Community Committee.
