Questions people ask.

What exactly is Hermes?+
The AI platform we install in your business — the nest your agents hatch into. It comes with hundreds of pre-installed skills, connects to the tools you already use, and learns how your business sounds. So the work it produces sounds like you, not like a chatbot.
What's an AI agent?+
A teammate in software form. Each agent has one job — chasing leads, writing documents, processing invoices, updating your website. You talk to it in plain English in Slack or Microsoft Teams, and it does the work. Because each agent only does its one job, it stays focused and never gets confused.
How much does it cost?+
Three tiers: Basic is A$10,000 and then A$499/month. Standard is A$25,000 with our agent marketplace to pick and hatch from, then A$499/month. Premium starts from A$50,000 for bespoke enterprise agents. Prefer not to own it? Rent the platform for A$949/month with no install fee — agents not included. AI usage is billed at cost through OpenRouter: you pay for what your agents actually use, not a per-seat licence.
Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude?+
Three reasons. Cost: those are fixed per-seat subscriptions you pay whether you use them or not — Hermes is pay-per-use, so quiet weeks cost almost nothing. Voice: generic chatbots sound generic — your agents learn your business, so everything sounds like you. Flexibility: when a better or cheaper model comes out, you swap it in with one click. You're never locked in. The comparison table has the whole argument.
Do I need to be technical?+
No. If you can send a message, you can use your agents. We install everything, connect your tools, and hand it over working. You can also build your own agents in plain English — no code — or ask us to hatch new ones for you.
Is my data safe? Do you need my passwords?+
Never a password — we connect through each app's official "Sign in with…" approval screen, and you can revoke any connection yourself, at any time, from your own admin settings. Hermes is installed for your business alone, each agent gets access only to the tools its job needs, nothing goes out without a person approving it, and your data is never used to train anyone's AI models. More in the security section.
What if I want out, or something breaks?+
Pause an agent, revoke a connection, or remove the app from Slack or Teams — all from your side, in seconds, no exit fee, and your data comes with you. If something breaks, your subscription covers it: we monitor, maintain and update your platform and keep your agents healthy. No tickets, no portals, no chasing.
