(03) — Why Hermes

The AI world is confusing on purpose. This isn't. Here's the whole argument, in plain English.

R / 01

Pay only for what you use.

ChatGPT and Claude charge a fixed subscription per person, every month, whether anyone uses it or not. Hermes runs on OpenRouter — you pay for the AI your agents actually use, by the token. Quiet week? Tiny bill. For most businesses that works out cheaper.

R / 02

It sounds like you.

The model is only half the story — the harness around it is what makes AI genuinely useful. Hermes ships with hundreds of pre-installed skills and learns your business: your tone, your templates, your way of doing things. ChatGPT sounds like ChatGPT. Your agents sound like you.

R / 03

Swap models with one click.

A new model just dropped and it's brilliant? Swap it in. Want to cut costs? Swap in a cheaper one. Your agents keep their memory and skills either way. You're never locked into one AI company again.

R / 04

One agent, job done.

Your invoice agent only talks invoices. Your sales agent only talks sales. No noise, no crossed wires, no thousand-message chat where everything blurs together. Complete separation — which is exactly why the work stays sharp.

Is it safe?

(07) — Security in plain English

You're about to let something read your invoices and send email as your company. That deserves a straight answer, not a page of acronyms. Here's the whole thing, explained the way we'd explain it to a friend.

Think of an agent as a new staff member — one who only ever gets the keys you hand them.

S / 01

It's your platform, not a shared one.

LikeYour own office — not a hot desk in a shared building.

Hermes is installed for your business and nobody else's. Your invoices, your emails and your documents don't sit in a pool with other companies' data. There is no shared filing cabinet that a mistake somewhere else could open.

S / 02

Each agent gets one set of keys.

LikeYour bookkeeper has the accounting login. They don't have the payroll one.

The invoice agent can reach your accounting software. It cannot touch your CRM, your website or your HR files — not because we asked it nicely, but because it was never given those keys. One job, one set of access.

S / 03

Nothing leaves the building without a human.

LikeA draft in your outbox. It's written, but you press send.

Agents do the work on their own — reading, matching, drafting, preparing. But anything that reaches a customer, moves money or goes public waits for a person to click approve. You saw it in the demo above. You decide where that line sits, agent by agent.

S / 04

We never ask for your passwords.

Like“Sign in with Google” — you approve it, and you can un-approve it.

We connect through the official, approved method each app already offers. You'll never type a password into anything of ours. The connection shows up in your own admin settings, and you can cut it off from there without asking us.

S / 05

Everything it does is written down.

LikeA visitor book at reception. Who came in, when, and what they did.

Every action an agent takes is logged in readable English: what it did, when, which file it touched, and who asked for it. If you ever need to explain something to an auditor, an accountant or a client, the trail is already there.

S / 06

Your data doesn't train anyone's AI.

LikeA lawyer reading your file — not a journalist reading it.

Your emails, invoices and documents are used to do your job and nothing else. They are not fed back to improve anyone's AI model, ours or the model providers'. That setting isn't buried in a menu — it's off, and it stays off.

S / 07

You can pull the plug yourself.

LikeDeactivating a staff pass. It stops working immediately.

Pause an agent, revoke a connection, or remove the app from Slack or Teams — all from your side, in seconds, without a support ticket. There's no hostage situation and no exit fee for turning something off.

S / 08

Real people are accountable for it.

LikeA tradesperson who gives you their mobile, not a ticket number.

We're an Australian company with two founders whose names and faces are further down this page. We maintain your platform, we patch it, and if something goes wrong you talk to us — not a chatbot, not a queue, not an offshore tier-one.

Questions people ask.

(09) — FAQ

What exactly is Hermes?+
Hermes is the AI platform we install in your business — it's where your agents live. It comes with hundreds of pre-installed skills, connects to the tools you already use, and learns how your business sounds. So the work it produces sounds like you, not like a chatbot.
What's an AI agent?+
A teammate in software form. Each agent has one job — chasing leads, writing documents, processing invoices, updating your website. You talk to it in plain English, and it does the work. Because each agent only does its one job, it stays focused and never gets confused.
Do I need to be technical?+
No. If you can send a message, you can use your agents. We install everything, connect your tools, and hand it over working. You just talk to your team.
How much does it cost?+
Choose the fit: own it with an A$10,000 one-off installation and then A$499/month, step up to A$25,000 including 10 agents, rent it for A$949/month all-in with nothing upfront and a 12-month minimum, or start from A$50,000 for enterprise bespoke agents. AI usage is billed at cost through OpenRouter: you pay for what your agents actually use, not a per-seat licence.
Why not just use ChatGPT?+
Three reasons. Cost: ChatGPT is a fixed subscription you pay whether you use it or not — Hermes is pay-per-use, so quiet weeks cost almost nothing. Voice: generic chatbots sound generic — Hermes learns your business, so everything sounds like you. Flexibility: when a better or cheaper model comes out, you swap it in with one click. You're never locked in.
Can I build my own agents?+
Yes — it's included with every installation, unlimited, and it's plain English rather than code. Prefer to hand it off? Pick ready-made agents from the library at set prices, or we'll build custom ones for you.
Slack or Microsoft Teams — do I have to pick one?+
No. Your agents work in both, and you can switch between them whenever you like — it's a setting, not a rebuild. Same agent, same memory, same skills; it just shows up in a different app. Some clients run Slack for the sales team and Teams for finance, and Hatchy sits in both. You can also talk to your agents in Hermes directly. Either way there's no new app for your team to learn.
Is my data safe?+
Hermes is installed for your business — it's your platform, connected to your tools, not a shared workspace with other companies. Each agent gets access only to the tools its job needs, nothing goes out without a person approving it, and your data is never used to train anyone's AI models. There's a fuller, jargon-free breakdown in the security section.
Do you need my passwords?+
Never. We connect through the official approval screen each app already has — the same "Sign in with…" flow you've used a hundred times. You approve the connection from your own account, you can see it listed in your admin settings, and you can revoke it yourself at any moment without asking us.
What if I want to switch it off?+
You pause an agent, revoke a connection, or remove the app from Slack or Teams — all from your side, in seconds. No support ticket, no notice period on turning something off, no exit fee. Your data stays yours and we'll hand back anything you ask for.
Will agents do things without asking?+
Only what you've told them to. Agents prepare work autonomously — drafts, reports, matched invoices — but anything that leaves the building waits for your approval, and anything you've put on a schedule runs exactly as you set it. You decide where the line sits, agent by agent.
What if something breaks?+
Your subscription covers it. We monitor, maintain and update your Hermes platform, keep your agents healthy, and support your team. No tickets, no portals, no chasing.
